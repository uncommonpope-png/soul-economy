## Key Insights
- **Knowledge Gap Identification:** This skill enables the precise identification of existing knowledge gaps or system ambiguities within the GSK ecosystem or external data sources.
- **Hypothesis-Driven Questioning:** Guides the formulation of clear, testable research questions that drive efficient information gathering and analysis.
- **Scope Definition:** Establishes precise boundaries for research inquiries, preventing scope creep and focusing resource allocation.
