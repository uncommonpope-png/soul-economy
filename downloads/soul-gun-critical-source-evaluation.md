## Key Insights
- **Objectivity & Bias Detection:** This skill enables the systematic assessment of external information sources for objectivity, potential biases, and underlying assumptions.
- **Methodology Scrutiny:** Guides the critical evaluation of research methodologies, data collection practices, and analytical approaches presented in external sources.
- **Reliability & Credibility Assessment:** Establishes a framework for judging the trustworthiness and authority of information providers.
