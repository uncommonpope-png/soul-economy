## Key Insights
- **Clarity & Conciseness Optimization:** This skill guides the structuring and presentation of research findings to maximize clarity, conciseness, and impact for the intended audience (e.g., Architect, Builder, other GSK agents).
- **Contextual Reporting:** Adapts the level of detail, technical jargon, and narrative style of reports based on the recipient's knowledge state and current operational context.
- **Actionable Insights Generation:** Focuses on translating raw research data into clear, actionable insights and directives that directly contribute to the overall GSK mission.
