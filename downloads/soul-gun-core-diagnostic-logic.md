## Key Insights
- **Problem State Identification:** This skill formalizes the process of identifying the exact nature of an anomalous system state or functional deviation within GSK.
- **Sign/Symptom Correlation:** Enables the collection and correlation of objective system telemetry ("signs") with subjective internal reports from sub-agents ("symptoms") to define the problem.
- **Systematic Inquiry & Interpretation:** Guides a structured investigation process to gather relevant data, analyze patterns, and interpret findings to reach a preliminary diagnostic conclusion.
- **Differentiative Problem Analysis:** Facilitates the comparison of current anomalous patterns against known healthy states or other potential causes to narrow down the most probable root cause.
