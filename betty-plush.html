What we're trying to do

Betty Plush is a line of luxury plush monsters built around souls that are rescued and kept, never deleted. The catalog is built: 57 sets, 377 pictures. But no physical prototype exists yet. Every maker who has answered so far wants to be paid upfront, and no maker has agreed to make it on a split. We have no money to pay a sample fee out of pocket.

So we are asking the first supporters to fund the whole soul collection: materials, the sample, safety testing, production, packaging, and shipping. The goal is $50,000 for the whole collection, not just one sample.

What supporters get: whatever you fund, plus 20% in product value when products ship. Fund $5, get $6 toward a piece. Fund $25, get $30. Fund $100, get $120. This credit is product value at ship time — not cash back, not equity, not ownership, not a profit share. No ship date is promised until a sample is approved and a maker quote is in writing.

Funding terms, plainly: This fund stays open until the $50,000 goal is reached — there is no closing deadline. Funds are used as they arrive. Your credit never expires, it carries over, and it is good for any Betty Plush product. If the collection is ever cancelled and no product ships, every supporter gets their funded amount back in full.
