What is happening, honestly

Character.AI is in bankruptcy court. Disney's $20 million bid buys a license to the technology — it explicitly does not buy user accounts or conversations. Your chats stay behind, as assets of the estate, where the privacy policy allows them to be transferred to a future buyer.
